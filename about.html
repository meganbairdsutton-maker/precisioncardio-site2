About

Built around a shared problem.

Cardiovascular science is becoming increasingly precise. But the systems that recognize, define and connect patients to disease-specific care have not always kept pace.

PrecisionCardio was founded to give therapeutics companies a place to work together on the barriers that recur across diseases and across companies.

01  Why we exist

Our mission

Help more patients reach a precise diagnosis and disease-specific care, sooner.

A broad cardiovascular presentation can reflect very different underlying diseases. Reaching the right diagnosis may depend on recognizing the clue, characterizing the phenotype, ordering the right disease-directed evaluation, interpreting the findings in context and connecting the result to appropriate care.

When one part of that pathway is missed, precision can stop short. PrecisionCardio exists to address the shared barriers that make that happen.

02  Why an industry alliance

Many of these challenges sit at the disease level, upstream of product-specific decisions.

They often require sustained work across companies and beyond them. PrecisionCardio gives members a structure to determine which shared problems warrant collective attention, organize the right expertise around them and move appropriate pre-competitive work forward.

03  Built to work beyond PrecisionCardio

The partner follows the priority, not the other way around.

Most meaningful initiatives will require expertise and reach beyond the member companies themselves. Members first determine the priority. PrecisionCardio then identifies the partners best positioned to help advance that work.

Chosen firstA member-selected priority
  • Professional societies
  • Patient organizations
  • Academic institutions
  • Clinical experts
  • Other organizations with relevant capabilities

Depending on the project, the partners may be any of these. None is a standing second class of membership.

04  What we are

An independent, member-governed nonprofit industry association.

For biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies working across precision cardiovascular medicine. Members set the priorities. PrecisionCardio provides the structure for pre-competitive collective action and the standing infrastructure needed to carry member-selected work forward.

05  The boundary is deliberate

At the disease level, the challenge is shared.

Product-specific decisions are not.

PrecisionCardio does not coordinate product-specific development decisions, product or commercial strategy, pricing, contracting or other competitively sensitive matters. Those decisions remain with each company.
