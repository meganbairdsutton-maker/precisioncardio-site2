About the Alliance
What is PrecisionCardio?
PrecisionCardio Industry Alliance is a nonprofit, member-governed industry association for biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies working across cardiovascular medicine. Members work together on shared, disease-level challenges that can appropriately be addressed pre-competitively.
Why does PrecisionCardio need to exist?
Different cardiovascular diseases require different science and different workups, but many of the barriers that keep patients from reaching a precise diagnosis and disease-specific care recur across the field. PrecisionCardio gives companies a structure to address those shared barriers collectively.
Is PrecisionCardio focused only on genetics?
No. Genetics is one important part of precision cardiovascular medicine, but disease-specific understanding may also come from phenotype, imaging, biomarkers, molecular characterization and other disease-directed evaluation.
How is PrecisionCardio different from a professional society or patient organization?
Professional societies represent clinicians; patient organizations serve patients and families. PrecisionCardio is an industry alliance. It does not replace those organizations and expects to work with them as strategic partners when their expertise is relevant to a member-selected initiative.
What is PrecisionCardio's legal status?
PrecisionCardio Industry Alliance, Inc. is a nonprofit industry association incorporated in Delaware. The Alliance is seeking recognition from the Internal Revenue Service as a tax-exempt business league under Section 501(c)(6).
The work
What kinds of work will members do together?
Members determine the priorities. Illustrative work could include professional-society education partnerships, clinician education, practical recognition or diagnostic resources, shared gap analyses, academic collaborations and other disease-level initiatives.
How are priorities chosen?
Members surface recurring challenges they are seeing and determine through the Alliance's governance process which ones warrant collective attention. The program of work is member-selected rather than predetermined by staff.
How are external partners selected?
The priority comes first. Once members select a shared question, PrecisionCardio identifies the professional society, patient organization, academic group, clinical experts or other strategic partners best suited to help advance that work.
What does “pre-competitive” mean?
It means companies collaborate on questions that can appropriately be addressed at the disease or field level without coordinating how individual companies compete. Disease-level challenges may be shared; product-specific decisions remain with each company.
What is outside PrecisionCardio's scope?
Product-specific development decisions, product and commercial strategy, pricing, contracting and other competitively sensitive matters remain outside the Alliance. Single-company promotional programs or work designed for the exclusive benefit of one member are also outside the model.
Membership and governance
Who can become a member?
Membership is intended for eligible biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies working in cardiovascular medicine. Professional societies, patient organizations, academics and clinicians participate as partners, advisors or collaborators rather than as industry members.
When does founding membership close?
Founding membership is open through December 2026. Founding members help shape the Alliance's initial priorities, governance and first program of work.
Who governs PrecisionCardio?
PrecisionCardio is governed by its members and Board of Directors. The founding Board is being formed with the founding membership, with detailed governance provisions set out in the Alliance's governing documents.
