01Our working definition

What is precision cardiovascular medicine?

Looking beyond the presenting diagnosis to identify the specific disease, mechanism, or cause driving it. A more precise diagnosis can change what happens next: risk assessment, treatment, surveillance, and care for families.

Heart failure is not one disease. Neither are many of the broad labels used across cardiovascular medicine.

05The boundary

At the disease level, the challenge is shared. Product-specific decisions are not.

PrecisionCardio works on shared, disease-level questions. Product-specific development decisions, product and commercial strategy, pricing, contracting, and other competitively sensitive matters remain with each company. Educational and scientific content is developed independently of member companies.
