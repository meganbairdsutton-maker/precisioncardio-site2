03Why an industry alliance

The science differs.The barriers repeat.

Different diseases require different workups.
When a necessary step is missed, the path to a precise diagnosis can stop.

Illustrative points of failure, not disease-specific claims

No therapy can help a patient who was never diagnosed.

04The boundary

At the disease level, the challenge is shared. Product-specific decisions are not.

PrecisionCardio works on shared, disease-level questions. Product-specific development decisions, product and commercial strategy, pricing, contracting, and other competitively sensitive matters remain with each company.
