MembershipBuilt by the companies shaping the field

Built by the companies shaping the field.

PrecisionCardio is member-governed. Companies do more than fund the Alliance: they surface what they are seeing, determine the shared priorities and contribute the expertise that moves the work forward.

01Who joins

Biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies.

Membership is designed for therapeutics companies working across precision cardiovascular medicine.

Professional societies, patient organizations, academic institutions, clinicians and other experts participate as strategic partners or collaborators when their expertise is relevant to a member-selected priority. They are not a second class of membership.

02What membership means

Members support the Alliance through annual dues and take part in its governance and program of work.

Surface recurring challenges they are seeing across the field.
Determine which issues warrant collective attention.
Contribute expertise through member-led working groups.
Help shape the Alliance's direction and initial program of work.

1Surface

Members bring forward recurring observations from across diseases, specialties and care settings.

2Select

Through member governance, the Alliance determines which issues should become shared priorities.

3Convene

A member-led working group forms around the priority, bringing in the relevant external expertise and strategic partners as needed.

4Advance

The group determines the appropriate collective action: professional-society collaboration, educational initiatives, practical resources, academic partnerships, shared gap analysis or other pre-competitive work.

04The partner follows the project

PrecisionCardio does not begin with a fixed roster of outside organizations.

Members determine the question first. The Alliance then identifies the professional society, patient organization, academic group, clinical experts or other partners best suited to help advance that specific work.

05Governance

Member-led by design.

PrecisionCardio is governed by its members and Board of Directors. The Board provides fiduciary and organizational oversight, while members help set priorities and participate in the work through the Alliance's governance structure and working groups.

Detailed voting, committee and governance provisions are contained in the Alliance's governing documents and membership materials.

06Clear pre-competitive boundaries

At the disease level, the challenge is shared.

Product-specific decisions are not.

PrecisionCardio does not coordinate product-specific clinical development, regulatory or commercial strategy, product positioning, pricing, contracting, market allocation or other competitively sensitive activity. Alliance activities will operate under appropriate antitrust and compliance procedures.
